Our story

Roots that run deep in Sialkot.

Ajmal Garden Nursery is one of those places people remember. Parents who bought their first garden plants here now return with their children and grandchildren, each generation finding a reason to linger among the leaves.

Over the years, the nursery has grown into one of Sialkot's largest and most trusted green destinations — known for an exceptionally broad collection, approachable guidance, and the pleasure of seeing the plants in person before taking one home.

Whether someone is starting small, refreshing a courtyard, planting a landscape or searching for an unusual specimen, the promise is the same: real choice and thoughtful, hands-on help.

A trusted place to begin, learn and keep growing — for every kind of plant lover.

Guidance that goes deeper

Specialist knowledge, shared generously.

Some plants ask for a little more understanding. These are the collections the team especially loves helping visitors explore.

01 / Bonsai

Small trees, patient craft.

Bonsai rewards observation over hurry. Ask the team about choosing a form, reading its shape and helping it settle into your space.

02 / Cacti & Succulents

Character in every form.

From sculptural cacti to tiny rosettes, discover low-fuss plants with remarkable personality — and learn what light and watering really mean for them.

03 / Exotic Plants

For the wonderfully uncommon.

If you are drawn to an unusual leaf, colour or growth habit, ask about the plant's home conditions and the small routines that keep it thriving.

The people behind the plants

A warm welcome is part of the tradition.

The nursery's experience lives in the conversations around a pot, a cutting and a new idea for the garden. Come by with a question — there is always something to learn together.
